Stat Compass 0.1.0 — Retail 12.1.0
Copy this StatCompass directory into Interface/AddOns only after separate
installation approval. Open the Character equipment page to see the panel.
The panel matches CharacterFrame height and shows four current-stat rows.
Rating bars require an explicitly verified rating cap and rating snapshot;
without that interface, current percentages remain visible and bars stay hidden.
Verified rating references can show minimum, arithmetic mean and maximum.
Raid/Mythic+ and Default/Flat dark are selected in the panel. Reset restores
Raid, Default, and minimap defaults. German is automatic for deDE; English is the fallback.
Drag the minimap button to move it. Left-click opens equipment.
Right-click opens Options > AddOns > Stat Compass: choose Default/Flat dark,
show/hide the minimap button, or reset appearance (keeps Raid/Mythic+).
The angle and visibility persist across reloads. No extra feature window or libraries.

IMPORTANT: This package contains no licensed EU top 50 data. Current stats are
shown; cohort minimum and maximum read Unknown. It does not provide meaningful target
recommendations until a properly sourced, licensed dataset is released.
